# Deep Shankar Pandey

I'm an Applied Scientist at Amazon, working on search and recommendation systems that help Fire TV customers discover content. My research focuses on **trustworthy AI**: building models that can learn from limited data and express uncertainty in useful ways.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computing and Information Sciences from Rochester Institute of Technology, where I worked with Prof. Qi Yu. My work spans evidential deep learning, Bayesian methods, uncertainty quantification, and few-shot learning. I'm also interested in large language models and making AI systems more reliable and useful in practice.
